01

Hamnet

Maggie O'Farrell

2020

Set in 1580s Warwickshire, "Hamnet" reimagines the family life behind one of William Shakespeare's most mysterious plays. The novel follows Agnes, a woman known for her unusual gifts, as she builds a life with her husband and their three children in Stratford. When their son Hamnet dies at eleven, the family is devastated by grief. Years later, that loss echoes in the writing of Hamlet. In lyrical, intimate prose, Maggie O'Farrell restores emotional depth to a story long left in the margins of history.
